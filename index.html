NYADW 2026 — Traces of Force

New York Asian Design Week presents Traces of Force, a group exhibition at Gallery 456 bringing together ten works by eleven designers across architecture and its intersections with furniture, photography, and painting. The exhibition examines how structural, technological, emotional, environmental, and social forces shape material form and spatial experience.

Organized through pairings and correspondences, the works place digital fabrication in dialogue with traditional craftsmanship, material tension with bodily resistance, and urban perception with emerging technology. Across the exhibition, Asian design emerges not as a fixed visual language, but through negotiations between precision and imperfection, inheritance and transformation, displacement and belonging.

The Exhibition

August 7–21, 2026
Gallery 456, New York

Traces of Force brought together ten works by eleven artists and designers across architecture, painting, photography, installation, and furniture design at Gallery 456, New York.

The inaugural Physical edition of NYADW's two-year dialogue between physical and digital design, the exhibition ran August 7–21, 2026, and was free and open to the public.

About NYADW

New York Asian Design Week (NYADW) is an annual cultural platform celebrating the diverse voices shaping contemporary Asian design in New York City and beyond. Founded by Lighthouse Global Foundation Inc., NYADW brings together architects, artists, designers, makers, and creative thinkers whose work reflects the evolving relationship between culture, identity, technology, and the built environment.

The event operates on a two-year curatorial cycle exploring two complementary themes: Physical and Digital. Each year focuses on one of these dimensions, creating an ongoing dialogue between tangible and virtual forms of design.
